Accessibility at the library

Step-free entrance and lift to all floors. Hearing loop at the help desk. Large-print books and audiobooks. Two computers with screen-reader and magnifier software. A quiet hour every Tuesday 10:00–11:00 with lights dimmed and no announcements.

FAQ

Who can join?
Anyone who lives, works or studies in Riverside. Bring one proof of address.
How long can I keep a book?
Three weeks. You can renew twice online or at the desk if nobody else has reserved it.
Are there late fees?
No. We removed late fees in 2024, but please return items so others can borrow them.
Can I suggest a book to buy?
Yes. Ask at the desk or email the library with the title and author.
